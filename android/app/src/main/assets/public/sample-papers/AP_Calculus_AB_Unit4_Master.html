Essential Unit 4 Formulas and Theorems
  • Meaning of the Derivative: f′(a) represents the instantaneous rate of change of f with respect to x at x = a. Units = (units of f)/(units of x).
  • Straight-Line Motion: Velocity v(t) = s′(t), Acceleration a(t) = v′(t) = s″(t). Speed = |v(t)|. Particle at rest when v(t) = 0.
  • Speeding Up vs Slowing Down: Speed is increasing when v(t) and a(t) have the same sign; decreasing when they have opposite signs.
  • Displacement vs Distance: Displacement = ∫_a^b v(t) dt = s(b) − s(a). Total distance = ∫_a^b |v(t)| dt.
  • Linearization: Tangent line approximation L(x) = f(a) + f′(a)(x − a). If f″(x) > 0, L(x) is an underestimate; if f″(x) < 0, L(x) is an overestimate.
  • Differential Approximation: dy = f′(x) dx, where Δy ≈ dy for small dx. Relative error = dy / y.
  • Related Rates Strategy: 1) Identify given rates and desired rate; 2) Relate variables with an equation; 3) Differentiate implicitly with respect to t; 4) Substitute numerical values.
  • L’Hospital’s Rule: For indeterminate limits of form 0/0 or ±∞/±∞, lim [f(x)/g(x)] = lim [f′(x)/g′(x)], provided the limit exists or is infinite.
