Essential Formulas & Integration Theorems
  • Average value: f_avg = (1/(b−a))∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx.
  • Net change theorem: accumulated change = ∫ₐᵇ rate(t)dt.
  • Motion: displacement = ∫ v(t)dt; total distance = ∫ |v(t)|dt; speed = |v(t)|.
  • Area between curves: ∫ (top − bottom)dx, or ∫ (right − left)dy.
  • Known cross sections: V = ∫ A(x)dx. Squares: s²; Rectangles: b·h; Semicircles: (π/8)d²; Equilateral triangles: (√3/4)s²; Isosceles right triangles: (1/2)leg².
  • Disk method: V = π∫ R(x)² dx (about horizontal axis) or π∫ R(y)² dy (about vertical axis).
  • Washer method: V = π∫ [R² − r²] dx or π∫ [R² − r²] dy, where R = outer radius and r = inner radius.
  • Shifted axis of revolution: radius is always distance from axis to boundary curve: |y_axis − y_curve| or |x_axis − x_curve|.
