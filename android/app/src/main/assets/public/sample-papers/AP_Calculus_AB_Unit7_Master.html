Core Differential-Equation Ideas
  • Exponential model: y′=ky gives y=Ceᵏᵗ. Positive k gives growth; negative k gives decay.
  • Equilibrium: For y′=f(y), constant solutions satisfy f(y)=0.
  • Slope field: At each point (x,y), the segment slope is the value specified by y′=f(x,y).
  • Separation: Rearrange to place y terms with dy and x terms with dx, integrate, then apply initial data.
  • Logistic model: P′=rP(1−P/K), with equilibria 0 and K; K is the carrying capacity and maximum growth occurs at P=K/2.
  • Logistic solution: P(t)=K/(1+Ae⁻ʳᵗ), where A=K/P(0)−1 for a positive initial population.
