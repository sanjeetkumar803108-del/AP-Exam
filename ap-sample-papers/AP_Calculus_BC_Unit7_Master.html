APPENDIX FORMULA REFERENCE & THEOREMS
Essential Unit 7 Rules
Core formulas, differential equation models, and numerical methods required for AP Calculus BC.
  • Separation of Variables: dy/dx = g(x) h(y) ⇒ (1/h(y)) dy = g(x) dx. Integrate both sides and immediately append the constant of integration +C before exponentiating or isolating y.
  • Domain of a Solution: The solution to an initial value problem is defined on the largest open interval containing the initial point x₀ on which both the function and its derivative exist and are continuous.
  • Exponential Growth & Decay: dy/dt = ky ⇒ y(t) = y₀ e^(kt). Doubling time / half-life: t = (ln 2) / |k|.
  • Newton's Law of Cooling: dT/dt = −k(T − Tₘ) ⇒ T(t) = Tₘ + (T₀ − Tₘ) e^(−kt), where Tₘ is the ambient temperature.
  • Euler's Method (BC Topic): xn+1 = xn + Δx, yn+1 = yn + f′(xn, yn) · Δx.
  • Euler / Tangent Line Error & Concavity: • If d²y/dx² > 0 (concave up), tangent segments lie below the curve ⇒ Underestimate. • If d²y/dx² < 0 (concave down), tangent segments lie above the curve ⇒ Overestimate.
  • Logistic Growth Model (BC Topic): dP/dt = k P (1 − P / M) or dP/dt = k P (M − P). • Carrying capacity: M = lim(t→∞) P(t). • Point of fastest population growth: P = M / 2 (inflection point of logistic curve). • Maximum rate of growth: (dP/dt)max = k M / 4. • Explicit solution: P(t) = M / (1 + A e^(−kt)), where A = (M − P₀) / P₀.
  • Mixing Tank Models: dS/dt = Rate_in − Rate_out = (c_in · r_in) − (S(t) / V(t) · r_out).
