🚀 Nibble & Slopes Style Screenshots - Quick Rules & Best Practices

1. The 3-Second Rule (Slide 1 Hook)

A user spends less than 3 seconds on the Play Store page. Slide 1 must state the ultimate dream benefit: "Score a 5 on Your APs" along with social proof (Rating / Courses).

2. Benefit-Driven Copy, Not Feature Names

Don't write "Chat Module". Write "24/7 Personal AI Tutor". Don't write "Camera OCR". Write "Instant Rubric FRQ Grader". Always explain how it saves their GPA.

3. High Contrast & Clean Device Bezels

Use modern curved phone frames with Dynamic Island and subtle drop shadows. Floating callouts (badges) draw the eye to key elements that might otherwise look tiny on mobile screens.

4. Google Play & App Store Specs

This studio outputs pixel-perfect 1080 × 1920 px (9:16) PNGs, which are 100% compliant with Google Play Console and scale cleanly for iOS (1242 × 2688 px).
