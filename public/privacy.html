🌟 100% Ad-Free & Zero Data-Selling Commitment

AP Exam App is built solely to empower students to succeed in Advanced Placement® courses and exams. We do NOT sell your personal data, we do NOT license your information to third-party data brokers, and we do NOT serve third-party behavioral advertisements.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how AP Exam App ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and safeguards the personal information of users ("you" or "Student") who download, access, or use our mobile applications (Android / iOS) and web services.

We operate in strict compliance with United States federal and state privacy statutes, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) principles, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other applicable state data protection frameworks.

2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Data Handling & Transparency

AP Exam App incorporates advanced multimodal artificial intelligence, including high-performance enterprise APIs provided by Google Gemini AI, to deliver instant step-by-step problem explanations, AP FRQ handwritten rubric scoring, and the AP Trap Radar™ distractor analyzer.

What Data is Sent to the AI Engine?

  • Text Inputs & Queries: Questions you type into AI Tutor chats, quiz drills, or notes summarizers.
  • Photographs & Handwriting Scans: Images of handwritten FRQ answers, notebook scratchwork, textbook diagrams, or typed homework problems captured via your camera or uploaded from your photo library.
  • Voice Transcriptions: Audio clips recorded during live AI Voice Tutor sessions (processed into text).

How Does the AI Process Your Data?

  • Zero Model Training Guarantee: Under our enterprise API terms with Google Cloud, your prompts, uploaded homework images, student handwriting photos, and voice transcripts are NEVER used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve Google's public foundation models or any third-party artificial intelligence systems.
  • Ephemeral Real-Time Inference: Data transmitted to the AI endpoint is processed strictly in-memory to generate the real-time pedagogical explanation or scoring rubric. Once the response stream concludes, the transient inference session is terminated.
  • Automatic Image Purging: Images uploaded for handwriting grading or problem scanning are temporarily buffered in secure cloud memory and automatically and permanently deleted within 1 hour of processing. We do not maintain any long-term repository of student photographs, handwriting samples, or biometric facial data.
  • No Biometric Extraction: Audio captured for the Voice Tutor is converted to transient phonetic text and immediately discarded; we never generate or archive voiceprints, biometric profiles, or speaker identification templates.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AP Exam App is designed for high school students, AP candidates, and lifelong adult learners. Our service is strictly not directed to children under the age of 13.

In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under 13. If we discover that an account has been registered by a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will immediately and permanently delete all associated account records and server files. Parents or guardians who believe a child under 13 has provided personal data may contact us at helpyou.ai.support@gmail.com for prompt manual deletion.

6. Student Educational Privacy (FERPA Alignment)

To the extent that school districts, educators, or students utilize AP Exam App for supplemental educational support, we treat student-generated study materials, quiz results, and diagnostic assessments as confidential educational records in compliance with FERPA principles. Student data is never commercialized, sold, or shared with advertisers.

7. State Privacy Rights (California CCPA / CPRA & Other US States)

Residents of California (pursuant to CCPA/CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and other states with comprehensive data privacy laws possess the following rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to request a complete disclosure of the specific pieces and categories of personal information we have gathered.
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and associated study data.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate profile details.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge differential pricing, or alter functionality if you choose to exercise your legal privacy rights.
  • "Do Not Sell or Share": We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law; therefore, no opt-out request is necessary.

8. Data Security & Storage

We maintain rigorous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

  • All data transmissions between your mobile device and our backend servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cloud databases are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Access to production databases is strictly restricted by authenticated security rules ensuring that students can only read and write their own personal study records.

9. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You maintain absolute control over your study data. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile Settings > Account Management > tap "Delete Account". This triggers an immediate, permanent purge of your profile, quiz logs, coins, notes, and session tokens from our cloud databases.
  • Manual Request: Send an email from your registered address to helpyou.ai.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We process all verified deletion requests within 48 hours.
