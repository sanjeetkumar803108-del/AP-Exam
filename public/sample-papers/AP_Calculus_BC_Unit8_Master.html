APPENDIX FORMULA REFERENCE & THEOREMS
Essential Unit 8 Rules
Core formulas, geometric area and volume theorems, and numerical methods required for AP Calculus BC.
  • Average Value of a Function: f_avg = (1 / (b − a)) ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx. By the Mean Value Theorem for Integrals, if f is continuous on [a, b], there exists c ∈ (a, b) such that f(c) = f_avg.
  • Net Change Theorem & Particle Motion: • Displacement: Δx = ∫_t₁^t₂ v(t) dt. Final Position: x(t₂) = x(t₁) + ∫_t₁^t₂ v(t) dt. • Total Distance Traveled: Total Distance = ∫_t₁^t₂ |v(t)| dt. • Speed = |v(t)|. Speed is increasing when v(t) and a(t) have the same sign (v(t)·a(t) > 0); decreasing when they have opposite signs.
  • Area Between Curves: • Slicing perpendicular to x-axis: Area = ∫ₐᵇ [y_top(x) − y_bottom(x)] dx. • Slicing perpendicular to y-axis: Area = ∫_c^d [x_right(y) − x_left(y)] dy.
  • Volumes with Known Cross Sections: Volume = ∫ A(x) dx (or ∫ A(y) dy), where: • Squares: A(x) = s². • Equilateral Triangles: A(x) = (√3 / 4) s². • Semicircles (diameter in base): A(x) = (1/2) π (s / 2)² = (π / 8) s². • Isosceles Right Triangles (hypotenuse in base): A(x) = (1/4) s². • Isosceles Right Triangles (leg in base): A(x) = (1/2) s².
  • Volumes of Revolution: • Disc Method: Volume = π ∫ [R(x)]² dx (or π ∫ [R(y)]² dy) when region directly abuts axis. • Washer Method: Volume = π ∫ [ (R(x))² − (r(x))² ] dx, where R(x) is distance to outer curve, and r(x) is distance to inner curve. • Shifted Horizontal Axis y = k: R(x) = |y_outer − k|, r(x) = |y_inner − k|. • Shifted Vertical Axis x = k: R(y) = |x_outer − k|, r(y) = |x_inner − k|.
  • BC Exclusive: Arc Length of a Curve: • As a function of x: L = ∫ₐᵇ √(1 + [f'(x)]²) dx. • As a function of y: L = ∫_c^d √(1 + [g'(y)]²) dy.
  • BC Exclusive: Parametric Arc Length & Planar Distance Traveled: • Distance Traveled = ∫_t₁^t₂ √( (x'(t))² + (y'(t))² ) dt = ∫_t₁^t₂ Speed(t) dt.
