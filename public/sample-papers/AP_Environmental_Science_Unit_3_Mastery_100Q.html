Essential Unit 3 Demographic Rules & Formulas
Core formulas, biogeochemical pathways, and environmental principles required for AP Environmental Science Unit 3.
  • The Rule of 70 (Doubling Time): Doubling Time (years) = 70 / r (where r is the annual percentage growth rate, e.g., if growth rate is 2%, doubling time is 70 / 2 = 35 years).
  • Population Growth Rates: Population Growth Rate (%) = [(CBR − CDR) / 10] or [(Births + Immigration) − (Deaths + Emigration)] / Initial Population × 100%.
  • Life History Strategies: r-selected species exhibit high biotic potential, rapid maturation, minimal parental investment, high fecundity, and Type III survivorship. K-selected species exhibit low biotic potential, extended parental care, delayed maturation, and Type I survivorship.
  • Replacement-Level Fertility: Exactly 2.1 children per woman in developed nations (the extra 0.1 compensates for infant and childhood mortality); typically 2.5 or higher in developing nations with high infant mortality.
  • Demographic Transition Stages: Stage 1 (Pre-Industrial: high CBR, high CDR, stable population) → Stage 2 (Transitional: high CBR, plunging CDR, explosive growth) → Stage 3 (Industrial: declining CBR, low CDR, decelerating growth) → Stage 4 (Post-Industrial: low CBR, low CDR, stable or negative growth).
