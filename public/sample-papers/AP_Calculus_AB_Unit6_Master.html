Essential Unit 6 Facts
  • Net change: if f′ is a rate, then f(b) = f(a) + ∫_a^b f′(t) dt.
  • FTC: if F′ = f, then ∫_a^b f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a).
  • Accumulation function: d/dx [∫_a^x f(t) dt] = f(x); with upper limit u(x), multiply by u′(x).
  • Riemann sums: left, right, and midpoint sums converge to the definite integral as n → ∞.
  • Trapezoid rule: T_n = (Δx/2)[f(x₀) + 2f(x₁) + … + 2f(x_{n−1}) + f(x_n)]; it equals (L_n + R_n)/2.
  • Integral properties: additivity over intervals, reversing limits changes sign, constant factors come out.
  • Motion: displacement = ∫v dt; total distance = ∫|v| dt; speed increases when v and a share a sign.
  • Average value: f_avg = (1/(b − a))∫_a^b f(x) dx.
  • Even/odd: over [−a, a], an even integrand doubles the half-interval integral and an odd integrand gives 0.
