• Justification:
1. Loss of Heterozygote Advantage: In a malaria-free environment, P. falciparum is absent; therefore, the HbA/HbA genotype suffers no malaria mortality (w₁₁ = 1.00) and is equal in fitness to the heterozygote (w₁₂ = 1.00).
2. Persistent Deleterious Homozygote Cost: The HbS/HbS genotype still causes severe sickle-cell anemia, resulting in reduced survivorship and reproductive output (w₂₂ = 0.20).
3. Directional Selection Against HbS: Because HbS no longer confers a compensating fitness benefit in heterozygotes, natural selection acts directionally against the deleterious HbS allele, progressively eliminating it from the gene pool whenever it is exposed in homozygotes.

FRQ 10: Topic 7.13: Origin of Life on Earth - Prebiotic Chemistry, Ribozymes, and Protocells Official Model Solution

★★★★★ VERY IMPORTANT
STEP-BY-STEP ANALYTICAL SOLUTION
Part ( (A)): • Miller-Urey Findings: Miller and Urey circulated inorganic reducing gases (H₂O, CH₄, NH₃, H₂) exposed to electrical spark discharges, successfully synthesizing diverse organic monomers, including amino acids (glycine, alanine) and carboxylic acids.
• Stage 1 Addressing: The experiment demonstrates that the fundamental monomeric building blocks of biological macromolecules can assemble abiotically from inorganic precursors under plausible prebiotic conditions without enzymes.
• Limitation: It addresses only Stage 1; it did not produce polymerized macromolecules (proteins, nucleic acids), self-assembling cellular membranes, or self-replicating hereditary genetic systems.
Part ( (B)): • In Vitro Molecular Evolution:
1. Variation: Round 0 contained 10¹⁴ randomized RNA sequences displaying diverse tertiary folded shapes.
2. Selection: Only ribozymes capable of catalyzing phosphodiester ligation bound substrates and were captured/amplified.
3. Iterative Adaptation: Over 10 rounds, catalytic velocity increased over 3.2 million-fold (from 0.00002 to 65 min⁻¹) while replication fidelity improved, proving that RNA populations undergo Darwinian evolution through mutation and selection.
• RNA World Dual Capacity:
- Informational Storage: RNA is composed of four nucleotides that encode hereditary information via Watson-Crick base-pairing (genotype).
- Catalytic Ribozymes: Single-stranded RNA folds into intricate tertiary conformations containing active catalytic sites (phenotype) that can catalyze replication and peptide bond synthesis. This resolves the paradox of DNA requiring proteins and proteins requiring DNA.
Part ( (C)): • Spontaneous Lipid Assembly:
- Amphipathic Structure: Fatty acids have hydrophilic polar carboxyl heads and hydrophobic nonpolar hydrocarbon tails.
- Hydrophobic Effect: In water, water molecules maximize entropy by excluding nonpolar tails, driving spontaneous self-assembly into spherical lipid bilayer vesicles (liposomes) with polar heads facing water and tails buried within the interior.
• Compartmentalization Benefits:
1. Solute Concentration: Traps macromolecules and substrates within microscale volumes, drastically increasing local concentrations and reaction kinetics.
2. Genotype-Phenotype Linkage: Prevents beneficial products of catalytic RNAs from diffusing away into the open environment; the benefits accrue directly to the encapsulated vesicle, enabling natural selection on individual protocells.
3. Electrochemical Gradients: The impermeable bilayer permits maintenance of internal pH and electrochemical proton gradients for primitive energy capture.
Part ( (D)): • Transition to DNA and Proteins:
- Ribozymes initially catalyzed primitive peptide synthesis (as in modern ribosomal rRNA).
- Once proteins took over enzymatic catalysis, reverse transcription copied RNA sequences into DNA.
• Biochemical Advantages:
1. DNA Advantage (Genome Storage): DNA lacks the reactive 2'-OH hydroxyl group present on ribose (possessing 2'-deoxyribose), making the phosphodiester backbone far more chemically stable and resistant to spontaneous alkaline hydrolysis. Additionally, double-stranded DNA provides a complementary template for high-fidelity repair.
2. Protein Advantage (Enzymatic Catalysis): Proteins are assembled from 20 chemically diverse amino acids (acidic, basic, polar, nonpolar, aromatic) compared to only 4 relatively uniform ribonucleotides in RNA. This provides vastly greater conformational flexibility, stereochemical versatility, and catalytic efficiency.
